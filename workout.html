A six-day split built for the men's physique standard: shoulders wide, back wide, waist small, everything tied together and dry. Legs are trained hard enough to be healthy and balanced — not hard enough to fight the board shorts for width at the hips.

Two hard pulling days sit at opposite ends of the week, and every one of them carries a set of lateral raises. Side delts are the widest-leverage muscle on a physique stage, so they get worked at some dose four days out of six.

Traps sit at the bottom on purpose. Heavy shrugging fills the gap between neck and delt and visually narrows the shoulder line — the opposite of what quarter turns reward. Two light sets keep the yoke healthy without building it.

Back at 26 is above the usual ceiling and it's a deliberate bet — it's split across two sessions and it's the second-biggest lever on the taper. If your pulling numbers stall two weeks running, take a set off each row before you touch anything else.

How it progresses

Double progression

Anchors

The lift marked ANCHOR is the one you chase. Hit the top of the rep range on every set at RIR 1 and add load next session — 5 lb upper body, 10 lb lower. Then work back up the range.

Accessories

Add reps before load. When all sets sit at the top of the range and the pump is still there, take the smallest jump the stack allows and rebuild.

Log everything

Load, reps, and RIR for every set. A program you can't compare week to week isn't a program — it's a workout you happened to do.

When you stall

Two sessions with no progress on an anchor means the problem is recovery, not the exercise. Sleep and food first, then swap the variation.

Weeks 1–3Accumulate

RIR 2 falling to 1. No intensity techniques. Add reps, add load, keep every session repeatable.

Weeks 4–6Intensify

RIR 1 falling to 0 on final sets. Drop sets and myo-reps where they're marked. This is where the week bites.

Week 7Deload

Half the sets, same loads, RIR 3–4. Keep cardio and posing. You come back stronger, not rusty.

Week 8+Reset

Swap each anchor for its cousin — hack squat to pendulum, incline dumbbell to incline machine — and start the block again.

Cardio & conditioning

Switches with phase

Cardio

Off-season. 2–3 sessions of 20–25 min low-intensity work, after lifting or on Sunday. 8–10k steps a day. Enough to keep conditioning and appetite honest, not enough to eat into recovery.

Prep. Start at 3 × 30 min low-intensity and add sessions before you cut calories again — up to 5–6 × 30–45 min, plus 1–2 short bike or sled intervals. 10–14k steps a day, tracked.

Posing

Off-season. 10 minutes, 3× a week — quarter turns and the relaxed front. It's cheap now and it exposes weak points a mirror won't.

Prep. 15–20 minutes daily, plus two full run-throughs a week filmed from the front and both sides. Holding a pose is a trained skill; conditioning you can't present doesn't score.

Fuel

Off-season. Protein around 1 g per pound of bodyweight, a small surplus — a quarter to half a pound a week. Carbs around training.

Prep. Give yourself 16–20 weeks. Aim for 0.5–1% of bodyweight a week; hold protein, pull carbs and fats. Keep carbs on Thursday and both back days — those are the sessions worth protecting.

Waist

No weighted side bends, no loaded twists, no heavy carries. Direct ab work stays flexion-based and moderate. Add five minutes of stomach vacuums most days — it trains the transverse to hold the midsection in and is exactly the control you need on stage.

Adjusting it

Read this before you change anything
  • One weak point per block. Add 4–6 sets to it and take those sets out of your best body part. Chasing three weak points at once means recovering from none of them.
  • Only five days available? Fold Day 5 into the others — biceps onto Day 6, triceps onto Day 2. Never cut Thursday.
  • Shoulders sore or clicking? Drop the upright row and the weighted dip first. Both are optional; lateral raises are not.
  • Not recovering? The full week is ~135 hard sets, which is a lot. Cut the last accessory from each day before you cut a day — that drops it to ~118 and usually fixes things inside two weeks.
  • Lower back cooked from Day 3? Swap the Romanian deadlift for a lying leg curl and the back extension for a reverse hyper.
  • Hips already wide? Keep legs at the prescribed volume and don't add. Width at the shoulder is what scores; width at the hip is what it's measured against.
  • Warm up properly. Five minutes of general work, then two to three ramping sets on the first exercise of each muscle. Ramp-ups don't count toward the set totals.
